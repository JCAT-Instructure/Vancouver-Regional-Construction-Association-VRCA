1. Updated BC Construction Labour Forecast (2026–2035)

BuildForce Canada's July 2026 forecast projects 48,200 new recruits required across BC to meet expansion and replacement demands, alongside 46,700 expected retirements (22% of the workforce). While 40,800 local young recruits under age 30 enter the workforce, the province faces a net 7,400 skilled worker deficit unless upskilling and employer-led retention are significantly accelerated.

Source: BuildForce Canada LMI BC Outlook (July 2026–2035)

Attract • Youth & School Pathways

VRCA Action: School & Pathways Programming

Engaging K-12 students, guidance counselors, and underrepresented talent pools with accessible trades previews and career discovery.

Canvas Enabler:

Open Catalog portal for public career discovery, video pathway previews, and introductory trade badges.

2

Prepare • Pre-Apprentice Readiness

VRCA Action: Industry Readiness Training

Equipping pre-apprentices with core jobsite safety fundamentals, trade math, tool safety, and basic work expectations before day one.

Canvas Enabler:

Self-paced mobile readiness modules, interactive safety assessments, and verifiable baseline tickets.

3

Enter • Technical Onboarding

VRCA Action: Apprentice & Site Orientation

Transitioning new hires onto active project sites safely with verified credentials, employer sponsorship tracking, and quick site access.

Canvas Enabler:

Canvas Credentials (Parchment) providing scannable QR code micro-badges for site superintendents.

4

Develop • Tech & Project Training

VRCA Action: Technical & Project Upskilling

Upskilling journeypersons and project teams in Mass Timber, BIM/Revit, Estimating, Procore, and BC Housing CPD compliance.

Canvas Enabler:

Offline-capable mobile learning, 5-minute tailgate modules, and stackable technical micro-credits.

5

Lead • Site Supervisors & Managers

VRCA Action: Leadership & Communication

Developing superintendents, foremen, and project managers in conflict resolution, crew management, and Gold Seal certification.

Canvas Enabler:

Cohort-based executive paths, video scenario assessments (Canvas Studio), and Gold Seal tracking.

6

Retain • Employer Analytics & Growth

VRCA Action: Employer Needs & Program Shaping

Giving general contractors visibility into crew skill density while gathering aggregate data to shape future VRCA course offerings.

Canvas Enabler:

Multi-tenant sub-account analytics providing real-time employer skill demand dashboards.

Technology Fit Deep-Dive

3. Practical Micro-Credentials, Field Learning & Employer Analytics

Addressing the three core areas highlighted in VRCA's review: delivering micro-credentials for construction tickets, field-ready mobile learning for active job sites, and aggregate analytics to shape future VRCA programming.

🎓

Practical Micro-Credentials

Transition from paper tickets to tamper-proof digital micro-badges (powered by Canvas Credentials / Parchment).

  • • Directly aligned with BC Housing CPD & Gold Seal credits
  • • Instant QR code verification for field site superintendents
  • • Stackable learning pathways supporting career progression
✓ Portable across all BC contractors & project sites
📱

Shorter Field Mobile Learning

Micro-learning delivered in 5-to-10 minute modules designed specifically for jobsite smartphones and tablets.

  • • Native offline mode for remote or underground jobsites
  • • Designed for morning tailgate talks & safety huddles
  • • Rapid video capture (Canvas Studio) for field demos
✓ Built for frontline trades with zero desk time
📈

Employer Skill Analytics

Multi-tenant sub-account analytics provide VRCA and employers with real-time intelligence on skill gaps and training demand.

  • • Contractor dashboards tracking employee safety compliance
  • • Aggregate data revealing regional skill shortages
  • • Continuous feedback loop to shape future VRCA courses
✓ Direct intelligence to shape future VRCA course design
